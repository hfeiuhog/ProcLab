# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and focused on the work. Critique claims and implementations with evidence. Avoid harassment, personal attacks and disclosure of private information.

Welcome different backgrounds. State uncertainty, identify synthetic/simulated results, attribute others' work and distinguish prototypes from production guarantees.

Maintainers may remove abusive or privacy-violating content and restrict repeated misuse. Use the available private reporting route for sensitive concerns; do not publish personal information.
